📋 How to Interpret These Results

Understanding the AI Analysis:

🎯 Confidence Scores & Thresholds:
  • Above threshold = DETECTED - The AI believes this condition is present
  • Below threshold = Not Detected - The AI believes this condition is absent
  • Optimal thresholds - Each disease has a different threshold (31%-76%) optimized for best accuracy
  • Higher confidence = More certain - 95% is more certain than 55%
🩺 What Each Disease Means:
  • Normal: No significant abnormalities detected
  • Diabetic Retinopathy: Damage to blood vessels in the retina from diabetes
  • Glaucoma: Damage to the optic nerve, often from high eye pressure
  • Cataract: Clouding of the eye's natural lens
  • AMD (Age-related Macular Degeneration): Deterioration of central vision
  • Pathological Myopia: Severe nearsightedness with complications
  • Other Diseases: Various abnormalities not fitting the above categories
⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Multiple conditions can coexist - An eye may have diabetes AND cataracts
  • 91.51% F1 score ≠ 100% - False positives and negatives can occur
  • This is a screening tool - Not a replacement for professional diagnosis
  • Optimal thresholds used - Each disease has a different detection threshold (not 50%)
